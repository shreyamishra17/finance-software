Import from Bank

Use your real browser — no automation, no special icons.

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: FIFO Import ★ (drag the button above to your toolbar)
  2. Open your bank in a new tab:
  3. Log in and navigate to your Transactions page. Scroll down to load as many transactions as you want.
  4. Click FIFO Import ★ in your bookmarks bar. You'll see an alert confirming how many transactions were imported.
  5. Come back to this tab and click Done — Load My Data below.

⚠ If you see "No transactions found", make sure you're on the Transactions page and transactions are visible on screen.
